Professional Profile

I lead mobile engineering teams that ship software people actually depend on. At Bosch, that's the Android diagnostics platform that tells a technician what's wrong with your car before you're stuck on the shoulder figuring it out yourself.

Somewhere in the last few years I went from writing the code to being responsible for whether it ships. It turns out I like the second job even more. I run the whole arc: requirements, design, delivery, monthly releases for customers in the US, Canada, and Mexico.

Hand me an unsolvable problem and I get quietly obsessed until it isn't. Lately that's meant modernizing our release pipeline and integrating AI frameworks into how we work day to day.

I believe in clean solutions over clever ones, and in failing fast.

I care about the craft. Kotlin, Compose, and anything at the intersection of mobile and machine learning will hold my attention longer than they should.

If you're building something interesting, or you just want to argue about mobile architecture, I'm at ankushwadhwa929@gmail.com.
